Monsters:
(S): Stationary --> doesn't move but does damage when touched.
(B): Bat --> moves on a predetermined path.
(F): Fairy --> moves randomly and is not fixated on the player.
(H): Hound --> has two turns, moves towards the player
(G): Goblin --> takes most direct path
(Z): Zombie --> moves towards the player in a random direction
(C): Coward --> runs from the player.
