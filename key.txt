Monsters:
(S): Stationary --> doesn't move but does damage when touched.
(B): Bat --> moves on a predetermined path. 
(F): Fairy --> moves randomly and is not fixated on the player.
(H): Hound --> has two turns, moves towards the player
(G): Goblin --> takes most direct path
(Z): Zombie --> moves towards the player in a random direction
(C): Coward --> runs from the player.

Buffs:
HealthPack --> heals 25 hit points.
TotemOfUndying --> restores the player to full health.
StrengthPotion --> increases the player's base damage by 5 permanently.
Whetstone --> permanently increases the player's base damage by 3.
MysteryBrew --> 50/50 chance of increasing health by 20 or dealing 15 points of damage.
MagicScroll --> finds spells in the game that th eplayer doesn't have yet and unlocks a new spell slot.
